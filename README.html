怎么使用 / How to use

在浏览器打开 index.html，点击封面播放；同一时间只播放一条。下载 MP4 上传到 Instagram 或 X，再复制对应英文文案。若复制按钮被浏览器限制，可直接从下方文本框选择文字。

Open index.html in a browser and click a cover to play with sound. Download the MP4, upload it to Instagram or X, and use the matching caption. If clipboard access is restricted, select and copy the text in the caption box.

另附 x-01.mp4 — x-20.mp4：720 × 1280 的 X 兼容版，与主版本为同一批 20 条影片。

The x-01.mp4 to x-20.mp4 files are optional 720 × 1280 copies of the same 20 films for X uploads.

表达与素材来源 / Visuals and source materials

光栅变化、立体层次与部分产品造型为原创动画概念，画面已标示 Effect illustration 或 Concept illustration。第 18 条以真实贺卡摄影为主，其他部分影片也使用真实产品照片；照片来自富晨产品摄影。动画示意不能替代实物打样或检测。

Optical changes, depth and some product forms are original illustrated concepts, labelled on screen. Film 18 focuses on real greeting-card photography; several other films also include product photographs from the FUCHEN product photography. Illustrations do not replace physical samples or testing.
