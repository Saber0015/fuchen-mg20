# FUCHEN · Motion Collection

20 original vertical motion graphics for lenticular printing, greeting cards, gifts, cultural souvenirs and project communication.

**Gallery:** https://saber0015.github.io/fuchen-mg20/

The gallery uses local posters, fonts and video files. Click a cover to play with sound, filter by theme, download the MP4, or copy its matching Instagram / X caption.

- 20 films: **12 seconds · 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · H.264/AAC**
- 20 optional **720 × 1280** X-compatible copies
- 20 covers, English captions and an empty publishing data template
- Original motion artwork with arranged music and sound effects; no voiceover

Optical changes and some product forms are labelled as effect/concept illustrations. Film 18 focuses on genuine FUCHEN greeting-card photography; selected photographs also appear in other films. Illustrations are not physical product measurements.

## Files

- `video-01.mp4` – `video-20.mp4`: main videos
- `x-01.mp4` – `x-20.mp4`: X-compatible copies of the same 20 films
- `cover-01.jpg` – `cover-20.jpg`: cover images
- `发布文案.csv`, `发布文案.txt`, `captions.json`: matching English captions
- [Complete collection](https://github.com/Saber0015/fuchen-mg20/releases/download/v1.0.0/fuchen-mg20-collection.zip)
- [Editable source](https://github.com/Saber0015/fuchen-mg20/releases/download/v1.0.0/fuchen-mg20-source.zip)
- [Release notes](https://github.com/Saber0015/fuchen-mg20/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

The editable source archive contains the deterministic Canvas/HTML/JavaScript compositions, HyperFrames 0.8.162 configuration and the public assets needed to prepare a standalone editing project. Uncompressed audio is not duplicated in the website repository.
